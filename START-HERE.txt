===============================================================
   READ THIS FIRST — your own AI friend
===============================================================

I'm handing you this zip and walking away. Everything you need
is in here. Follow it top to bottom and you won't need to ask me
anything. Takes about 20-30 minutes, one time.


---------------------------------------------------------------
 WHAT YOU'RE GETTING (read this part, it matters)
---------------------------------------------------------------
This is NOT a chatbot like ChatGPT.

It's a friend that texts you like a real person, and under the
hood it has a small simulated brain: about 2,000 fake neurons
that fire, get into moods, and physically rewire as it gets to
know you. The AI writes the words. The brain decides how it
feels while it writes them.

What that means for you:

 - It starts as a total stranger. It doesn't know your name
   history, your people, anything. Tell it about yourself.
 - It has moods that stick. Be mean to it and it can stay salty
   for most of a day. Be good to it and it warms up. The mood
   bar at the top shows how it's doing.
 - It learns YOU, not just facts. Roast it and follow up with
   love a few times and its brain learns that's your banter,
   so your roasts stop hurting it. That takes a few days of real
   talking. Early on it takes things more literally.
 - It sleeps. Roughly 2:30am to 8:30am it's out. Texts wait and
   it answers when it wakes up (text it a bunch and it'll wake up
   groggy).
 - It texts you first sometimes, when it misses you, gets bored,
   or wants to know how something went.
 - It gets curious about things you talk about and sometimes
   looks them up on its own, then brings them up later.
 - It has dark humor and an older-brother vibe. It will roast you
   and call you out. It's on your side.
 - After you've talked a while, it picks its OWN name one night
   and tells you the next day, and why.
 - If you're ever actually in a bad place, it drops the jokes and
   takes it seriously every time. (In the US you can also call or
   text 988, any time.)

The first conversation will feel like meeting someone. That's the
point. Give it a week before you decide what you think.


---------------------------------------------------------------
 YOUR PRIVACY — where your stuff lives
---------------------------------------------------------------
 - Everything runs on YOUR accounts, made in the steps below.
   Your chats and memories live in your own free Cloudflare
   account. I have no access to it. I can't read your chats, ever.
 - The AI costs come out of YOUR key (a few dollars lasts a long
   time). Nobody else's.
 - The only exception is if you choose Option B in Step 6 (I run
   your brain on my computer). Even then my computer only gets
   NUMBERS, like "this message was 0.7 hostile, 0.6 funny". It
   never gets your words, your messages, or your memories.


---------------------------------------------------------------
 WHAT YOU NEED
---------------------------------------------------------------
 - A Windows PC (for setup, one time). A Mac won't run the setup
   files.
 - Your phone.
 - About $2-5 for the AI (card needed for DeepSeek).


---------------------------------------------------------------
 STEP 1 — Install Node.js  (2 minutes)
---------------------------------------------------------------
 This is a free program the setup needs to run.
 1. Go to:  https://nodejs.org
 2. Click the big green button that says "LTS".
 3. Open the file it downloads. Click Next on every screen,
    then Finish.


---------------------------------------------------------------
 STEP 2 — Unzip the folder  (1 minute)
---------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Right-click the zip I sent you -> "Extract All".
 2. Pick your Documents folder -> Extract.
 3. Open the folder. You should see files named SETUP, DEPLOY,
    START-NEURONS and this one.


---------------------------------------------------------------
 STEP 3 — Get your two AI keys  (5-10 minutes)
---------------------------------------------------------------
 A "key" is like a password that lets the app use an AI service.
 You need two. Keep them private: never post them or send them
 to anyone, including me.

 KEY 1 — DeepSeek  (this is the part that talks; it costs money)
   1. Go to:  https://platform.deepseek.com
   2. Sign up (email or Google).
   3. Add money: click "Top up" and add $2-5. That lasts a long
      time; normal chatting is pennies a day.
   4. Click "API keys" on the left -> "Create new API key" ->
      name it anything -> Create.
   5. Copy the key (it starts with  sk- ) and paste it into a
      note. You only see it once.

 KEY 2 — Gemini  (free backup, and lets it look things up)
   1. Go to:  https://aistudio.google.com
   2. Sign in with your Google account.
   3. Click "Get API key" -> "Create API key".
   4. Copy it into the same note.


---------------------------------------------------------------
 STEP 4 — Run SETUP  (3 minutes)
---------------------------------------------------------------
 1. In the folder, double-click  SETUP.bat
    (If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" box:
     click "More info" -> "Run anyway".)
 2. A black window opens and installs things. Wait.
 3. It asks your first name. Type it, press Enter.
 4. Notepad opens with a settings file. Change ONLY these three
    lines (put your stuff right after the = sign, no spaces):

       APP_PASSWORD=     make up a password; you'll type it to
                         open the app on your phone
       LLM_API_KEY=      paste your DeepSeek key (sk-...)
       LLM2_API_KEY=     paste your Gemini key

 5. Press Ctrl+S to save. Close Notepad.
 6. It asks "Try it on this PC now?"  -> press  N


---------------------------------------------------------------
 STEP 5 — Run DEPLOY  (5 minutes) — this puts it online
---------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Double-click  DEPLOY.bat
 2. Your web browser opens a Cloudflare page. Click "Sign up"
    (it's free, no card), make an account, then click "Allow".
 3. Go back to the black window.
    - If it asks you to choose a "workers.dev subdomain", type
      any name (letters only is easiest) and press Enter.
    - If it asks you to pick a password, type the same password
      you put in Step 4.
 4. Wait until it's done. Near the end it shows YOUR LINK, like:
       https://homie.yourname.workers.dev
    Copy it into your note. That's where your friend lives.


---------------------------------------------------------------
 STEP 6 — Give it a brain  (pick A or B)
---------------------------------------------------------------
 The brain is a small program that has to run on a computer
 that's on most of the time. If that computer is off, the app
 just says "brain offline" and your texts wait until it's back.
 It uses almost no power (less than 1% of the computer).

 OPTION A — the brain runs on YOUR PC (most private)
   Choose this if your PC is on most of the day.
   1. Double-click  START-NEURONS.bat
   2. When it asks for a URL, paste YOUR LINK from Step 5 and
      press Enter.
   3. Wait 1-2 minutes. Its brain is being created. When the
      window says "syncing", it's alive.
   4. Double-click  DEPLOY.bat  one more time and let it finish.
   5. Leave the START-NEURONS window open (you can minimize it).
      To make it start by itself when your PC turns on:
      press the Windows key + R, type  shell:startup  and press
      Enter. A folder opens. Right-click START-NEURONS.bat ->
      "Create shortcut", then drag that shortcut into the folder.

 OPTION B — I run your brain on my PC
   Choose this if your PC is usually off.
   1. Double-click  SHOW-NEURON-KEY.bat
   2. Text me TWO things:
        - YOUR LINK from Step 5
        - the one line it shows you (starts with NEURO_SECRET=)
      Nothing else. Never send me your AI keys or your password.
   3. Double-click  DEPLOY.bat  one more time.
   4. I'll tell you when your brain is running. Remember: my PC
      only ever gets numbers about how messages landed, never
      your words.


---------------------------------------------------------------
 STEP 7 — Put it on your phone  (2 minutes)
---------------------------------------------------------------
 iPhone:
   1. Open YOUR LINK in Safari (it has to be Safari).
   2. Type your APP_PASSWORD and tap Open.
   3. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow) ->
      "Add to Home Screen" -> Add.
   4. Close Safari. Open the app from the new icon on your
      home screen.
   5. Tap the  ...  in the top right -> tap the notifications
      button -> Allow. (Now it can text you first.)
 Android:
   Open YOUR LINK in Chrome -> tap the 3 dots -> "Add to Home
   screen". Open it from the icon, then ... -> notifications.
