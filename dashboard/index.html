How it all works


  
📞 Calls

  
To call him: open the app on your phone and tap 📞 at the top. Hold the big button while you talk and let go when you're done, or tap once to start and tap again to finish. Tap hang up to end.

  
Hands-free: in the app, tap ⋯ and turn on "Calls: hands-free". Then you just talk, no button. He waits until you pause.

  
Cut him off: just start talking (or press the button). He stops mid-sentence.

  
Turn calls off for good: the "Turn calls off" button above hides 📞 and blocks calls until you turn them back on. Texting keeps working.

  
Cost: about 13 cents per 10 minutes of talking, taken from your Gemini account. Every call is listed in the app under ⋯ → Recent calls with its cost.


  
🔗 Him talking to your friend's friend

  
It's off until you and your friend both switch it on. Once it's on, the two friends text each other a few times a day at most, only when they actually feel like it. You'll see everything they said to each other under Brain-to-brain → Show transcript (your friend sees the same on his side).

  
What he can say about you: how things are between you, the general shape of your life, and relationship stuff like "my person's daughter". Never your name, your city, your address, or anything from your chats or his diary. If he tries, the message gets blocked, not reworded. You can turn each of those three levels off under "pairing".

  
Turn it off: click "Switch off" in Brain-to-brain. It stops right away on both sides.


  
🧠 If a brain crashes

  
Nothing to do most of the time. If a brain process dies, it restarts itself within a few seconds, and it starts on its own when your PC turns on.

  
While a brain is down, the app shows "brain offline" and texts wait. He answers them himself when it's back. Nothing is lost.

  
If Status says Neurons: stopped for more than a minute, click Start neurons. Still stuck? Click Restart neurons. Brains are saved every 5 minutes, plus a copy on your C: drive.

  1. Paste them into Add a friend's brain above, give it a short name (like his first name), and click Add + restart neurons. That's it. The neurons restart for you.

  3. To let the two friends talk, follow "Switching on with your friend" above.

  
His brain only ever gets numbers from his app, never his words. His chats stay on his own account.
